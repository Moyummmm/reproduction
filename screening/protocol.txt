Title/abstract triage protocol v2, frozen 2026-11-05.
Population: all records in search_protocol/records.csv after its documented exact-identifier deduplication. One automated model screens every record; this is not independent human screening and is not a final eligibility determination.
Question: does the title or abstract suggest original research that uses an LLM substantively for a security analysis, testing, verification, vulnerability discovery, repair, or protection task that targets operating-system components or privileged interfaces (e.g. kernel, drivers, syscalls, OS filesystem, OS network stack, system services, hypervisors or TEEs)? General software work, security of LLMs, and OS engineering without a security outcome are not direct core evidence, though they may be adjacent.
For each record return exactly one of: POSSIBLE_CORE when the available title/abstract suggests all core dimensions; EXCLUDE when the available evidence clearly demonstrates at least one dimension is outside scope; UNCLEAR when evidence is missing, ambiguous, or insufficient, including when a missing abstract makes scope or evaluation uncertain. Preserve any potentially relevant paper as UNCLEAR rather than invent details. A title-only candidate cannot be POSSIBLE_CORE if the security task or LLM role is not explicit. Reviews and surveys are EXCLUDE from the core empirical research set. Neither POSSIBLE_CORE nor EXCLUDE certifies full-text eligibility or is independently reviewed.
Publication-year scope is nominally 2020–2026 through the 2026-11-05 search. Do not treat the provided year as a reliable exclusion: it may refer to a different version, and the exact cutoff date is not available for every record. A separate deterministic field will label the supplied year as in_range, outside_range, or unknown; version dates require later verification. Judge the topical question independently of year.
Each result includes a short evidence-grounded reason and a brief exact supporting excerpt from the provided title or abstract. Do not use outside knowledge or invent evidence. Results failing schema, cardinality or ID checks are failures, never silently discarded. Model: deepseek/deepseek-flash via OpenCode; model snapshot and generation parameters were not exposed by the client. Raw requests and JSON event streams are preserved per batch. Record source fields and abstracts are snapshots and may be incomplete or contain extraction errors.
