K-Repro and SyzHarness both report 100 KernelCTF cases, but the papers describe different collection cutoffs and model/budget settings. The survey compares their workflow design without treating their rates as a controlled head-to-head comparison. Likewise, K-Repro's lack of a statistically significant cutoff-group difference and Live-kBench's repair-task differences motivate task-specific temporal evaluation; neither result directly establishes a model's training-set membership.
