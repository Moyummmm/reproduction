# Targeted addendum: LLM-assisted kernel exploitation

The two provider reports below were added during targeted follow-up on 6 November 2026. They are separate from the frozen historical and 225-record candidate inventories; adding them does not alter those counts. The linked reports are the primary sources. Their results are provider-reported and should be interpreted with their stated evaluation conditions and disclosure status.

- **OpenAI, “A Thousand Fewer Paths to Root,” 4 November 2026.** [Primary report](https://openai.com/index/a-thousand-fewer-paths-to-root/). The described pipeline analyzed 2,308 candidate findings and dynamically reproduced 1,724 on a Linux distribution. The report's *Exploitability assessment* section records 1,169 working local privilege-escalation exploits. After variant analysis, the authors developed 948 distinct patches and used that count to determine 948 unique LPE vulnerabilities. The manuscript follows this detailed accounting; the 1,724 dynamic-validation results are not labeled as a deduplicated total. The mid-October run cost 1,213,430 US dollars in API credits. Candidate patches and exploits were subject to human review before reporting. The figures are not a count of publicly disclosed CVEs or proof that all generated patches were accepted upstream.
- **Anthropic Frontier Red Team, “Assessing Claude Mythos Preview's Cybersecurity Capabilities,” 7 April 2026.** [Primary report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/mythos-preview). The report describes Linux local privilege-escalation chains involving multiple vulnerabilities. It also reports that a separate set of remotely triggerable memory vulnerabilities resisted the model's exploitation attempts. Several technical details were withheld pending coordinated disclosure; the report must not be interpreted as a general success rate across operating systems.

The manuscript uses these reports as examples of exploitability assessment and candidate patch generation, not as a common benchmark or independent verification of all vendor claims.
