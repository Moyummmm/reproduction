\section{Guide to the Candidate-Record \revise{Inventories}}
\label{app:candidate-inventory}
\revise{The candidate inventory (Supplementary Table S1) contains 225 merged records with source identifiers, bibliographic information, preliminary scores and evidence-status fields. Record identifiers refer to inventory entries, not unique studies or bibliography numbers.}

\revise{The historical inventory (Supplementary Table S2) preserves the 220 rows scored 1--3 in the available archive. The later 225-record set is a different selection with revised scores; the counts are not consecutive screening stages. The crosswalk and matching notes document source relationships and publication versions.}

\revise{Search queries and timestamps for the top-20 Crossref update are in \url{reproduct/search/search_metadata.csv}. The later top-100 Crossref/arXiv audit and arXiv pagination are documented under \url{reproduct/search_protocol/} and \url{reproduct/arxiv_pagination/}. Preliminary model decisions and protocol are in \url{reproduct/screening/}; citation mappings and source checks are in \url{reproduct/audit/}.}

\revise{The claim-to-citation audit and later-verified literature addendum are documented in \url{reproduct/revision/claim_citation_audit.md} and \url{reproduct/revision/literature_addendum.csv}.}
