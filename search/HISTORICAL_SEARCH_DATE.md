# Initial Google Scholar search date

The date of the initial Google Scholar literature search, **10 March 2026**, was supplied during the manuscript revision on 7 November 2026. Section III of the revised manuscript reports this date alongside the 18 saved keyword-based query formulations.

The original query execution logs and result lists were not retained in the research archive. The date above is the date supplied during revision, not a date reconstructed from query-level logs. `historical_query_formulations.txt` is preserved as an earlier record; its statement that dates were not established reflects the information available when that file was prepared.
