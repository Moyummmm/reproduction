# Record inventories and reconciliation

The 225-record candidate inventory is a merged, rapidly screened candidate set, not a deduplicated set of eligible studies. `supplementary_candidate_inventory.csv` preserves the workbook's 26 candidate fields, adds the source legacy row and historical score where applicable, and does not overwrite preliminary assessments. `supplementary_legacy_inventory.csv` contains the 220 rows with scores 1, 2 or 3 in the original 485-row CSV. The original scores are preserved alongside the later reclassification. The 220 and its 45/116/59 score distribution match the historical manuscript totals; without the historical submitted supplementary file, this agreement alone does not prove that the exact submitted set has been recovered.

`legacy_to_candidate_crosswalk.csv` traces all 485 original rows. A direct mapping uses the candidate workbook's explicit legacy row identifier, not automated title matching. Two previously documented version links (31 to 45 and 223 to 222) are marked provisional; row 31's identity is an inference from contextual evidence. Of the 220 scored historical records, 92 have a direct source-row link, one has a provisional link, and 127 have no direct link. An absent link means only that the record is not represented by that source-row identifier in this candidate set. It is not an exclusion decision. The candidate set has 130 records with direct legacy row identifiers and 95 new candidate records; six new search records were linked to existing legacy rows in the supplied workbook and are not additional rows.

`potential_duplicate_pairs.csv` records pairs identified by exact normalized title, provided DOI, or arXiv ID in article links. The scan is incomplete and does not automatically merge rows. Three pairs have been checked: L094/L130 cite arXiv:2411.11532; L103/L116 cite arXiv:2405.17238; C0631/C0632 correspond to v1/v2 of the same Research Square preprint. Three pairwise comparisons within C0041/C0042/C0046 remain unresolved: C0042's DOI resolves to the Research Graph post, but the other DOI relationships require verification. All rows remain in the 225-record file. More duplicates and version links may remain, so no unique-study count is asserted.

Sources in `sources/` are local, offline copies of the necessary fields: `legacy_original.csv` from `relatedfiles/old/papers - deduplicated_papers.csv`; `legacy_reclassified_source.csv` from sheet `总表` of `relatedfiles/old/LLM4OSSec_文献整理表.xlsx`; and `candidate_source.csv` from sheet `合并候选` of the enriched workbook in `outputs/01a1aa51-9c49-7940-bc79-2ed294ab04f3/`. They preserve the data values from those sheets and omit workbook metadata and unrelated sheets. They do not reconstruct the original query execution logs, screening decisions or independent-review procedures. The initial search date was supplied during revision; see `../search/HISTORICAL_SEARCH_DATE.md`.

From the `reproduct` directory, run `python3 inventory/build_inventory.py` to regenerate the four CSVs using only Python's standard library and these local sources. UTF-8 with BOM is used to support spreadsheet applications. Regeneration overwrites the four derived CSV files; the source copies are unchanged. Original source files in the project remain unchanged.
